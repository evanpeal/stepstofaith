Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Steps to Faith is a Bible reading app. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. If you have any questions, email stepstofaithapp@gmail.com.

Using the app without an account

You can use Steps to Faith without signing up. In that case your progress (lessons, streaks, gems, and reflections) is saved only on your own device and is never sent to us.

Information we collect if you create an account

  • Email address and password so you can sign in. Passwords are handled securely by our login provider and we never see them.
  • Name and profile details you choose to add, like a display name, avatar, favorite verse, church, or phone number. Your phone number is only used so friends who already have it can find you.
  • Your progress, such as completed lessons, streaks, gems, and reflections, so it stays in sync across your devices.
  • Things you post in Upper Room, including messages, prayer requests, reactions, rooms you create, and friend connections.

How we use it

We only use your information to run the app: signing you in, saving your progress, and letting you connect with friends and rooms. We do not sell your information, we do not show ads, and we do not use third party tracking or advertising tools.

Who can see your information

Your name and avatar can be seen by friends and by members of rooms you join. Posts and messages are visible to the people you share them with. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Where it is stored

Account data is stored with Supabase, a secure database provider. The website is hosted by Vercel. These companies only process data to keep the app running.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time. Open the app, go to Profile, tap Delete account, and confirm. This permanently removes your account, progress, posts, messages, friend connections, and any rooms you own. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Children

Steps to Faith is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
